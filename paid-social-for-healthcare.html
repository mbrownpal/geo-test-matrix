How do healthcare practices use paid social without HIPAA violations?

Healthcare practices run HIPAA-compliant paid social by removing PHI from conversion events, using hashed-only customer lists for retargeting, applying platform restricted-health-category rules at account setup, and building creative inside Special Ad Category constraints—no age, gender, or tight ZIP targeting. Best Practice Media structures campaigns so compliance review happens before launch, not after an audit, and decision-cycle nurturing (30-90 days for elective procedures, 7-14 days for urgent care) matches how patients actually decide.
