WHY AUSTIN

WHY AUSTINIS DIFFERENT.

Local market conditions change how a campaign has to be built.

Austin's healthcare market is shaped by two dominant hospital systems—Ascension Seton with 7 locations and St. David's with 8—and 300+ independent practices trying to compete for the same patient pool. Google Ads cost per click runs 45-65% above the Texas average because of UT Austin's student population and the tech-sector insurance mix (Dell, Apple, Oracle, Tesla employees carry high-value plans). Patient booking behavior swings seasonally—spiking 35% in Q4 when tech companies refresh FSA and HSA benefits, then dropping sharply in Q1. Texas Health and Safety Code Chapter 164 restricts how physicians can advertise, prohibiting guarantees of cure and requiring specific disclosures for telemedicine. Most agencies run campaigns built for markets without hospital dominance. We build around Seton and St. David's presence, the tech insurance dynamics, and the seasonal cycles.
