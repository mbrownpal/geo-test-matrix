On The Ground

Why AustinIs Different.

Local market conditions change how a campaign has to be built.

Austin's dental market runs denser than the city's population suggests. Eight hundred practices compete for search inventory across downtown, South Congress, and Domain. Google Ads cost per click sits 40-60% above the Texas average because of UT Austin's student population and the tech-sector insurance mix. Patient booking behavior swings seasonally—spiking 30% in Q4 when companies refresh FSA and HSA benefits, then dropping in January and February. Texas advertising rules narrow what a practice can say: no superiority claims, no before-and-after photos without specific disclaimers. Most agencies run campaigns that work elsewhere and get flagged here. We build around the rules, the seasonality, and the competitive density.
