WHY DENVER

WHY DENVERIS DIFFERENT.

Local market conditions change how a campaign has to be built.

Denver's dental market is shaped by University of Colorado School of Dental Medicine, which operates teaching clinics that compete on price and force independent practices to differentiate on convenience and service quality instead. Five hundred practices compete across Cherry Creek, Capitol Hill, and Highlands, and UCD produces 60+ graduates annually who often open local practices—intensifying competition every year. Google Ads cost per click sits 35-50% above the Colorado average because of Denver's high median income and employer-sponsored insurance penetration (tech and energy sector). Patient booking behavior swings seasonally—spiking 30% in Q4 when companies refresh FSA and HSA benefits, then dropping 20% January-February when families prioritize ski-season spending over dental care. Most agencies run campaigns that ignore the teaching clinic dynamic and waste budget competing on price. We build around the quality-versus-price split, the Q4 benefit cycle, and the ski-season spending gap.
